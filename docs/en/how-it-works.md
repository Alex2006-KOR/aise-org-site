# How It Works

If [Background & Philosophy](/en/background) was "why," this category is **"how."** Five pages
start from the static structure and move steadily toward the parts that move.

## Why read them in this order

1. **[The shape of the org](/en/organization-model)** — first, **what exists**. How staff,
   departments, and roles are laid out, and why line depth was capped at 2. Every name shows up
   here, so this page has to come first.
2. **[Staff & governance](/en/staff-governance)** — the position on that layout that needs the
   most explaining is the staff one. Why execution accountability was split from operational
   accountability, and why one staff figure became three.
3. **[A department's lifecycle](/en/lifecycle)** — **here the structure starts moving.** The time
   axis of a single department, from formation to end.
4. **[How collaboration works](/en/collaboration-model)** — after the time axis comes **several
   things running at once**. How collaboration gets assembled inside a department and between
   departments.
5. **[Operator vs Meta Mode](/en/operator-vs-meta-mode)** — last comes the safety mechanism. If
   the four above are "doing work," then the moment the organization **changes itself** has to be
   separated from that.

In one line: **what exists → who manages it → what happens over time → what happens in parallel
→ what happens when the structure itself changes.**
