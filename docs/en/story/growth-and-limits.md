# 7. Growth, results, limits

::: lead
Growth accumulates not in an individual role but in what the organization can do (the portfolio) and in the common
templates. Results are still TBD, and what is unsolved is collected in this page's limits section.
:::

## Growth: it accumulates in the organization, not in a role

::: point At first I pictured "people (roles) developing themselves through the knowledge they gain in projects". I dropped that approach. {#people-growth}
Had I modeled people's growth literally, the persona file of the "person" hired into a role would have grown longer and
longer. That increases the fixed cost, and that persona is not needed for every task. So I boldly changed the approach.
:::

::: point Instead, roles are hired and fired. When a department requests the staff it actually needs, HR-staff researches, checks and approves it. Using a role as a per-department instance also compensates for the problem.
Many departments use the same role definition, and the domain context each department needs is attached separately to
that department's records, as a domain profile researched by HR-staff. Managing people is HR-staff's job, so the PM
does not manage people — thanks to that split of responsibility, the PM no longer has to carry "people's minds and
growth".
:::

::: point So what is growth: the common templates getting stronger through knowledge gained in projects, and roles getting listed in the portfolio and used again and again. That is what I regard as growth. {#what-growth-is}
- The portfolio: the list of reusable roles, workflows and insights, promoted by the rule "prove it first, then promote".
- The common base and templates: strengthened in Meta mode.
- Both get their material from the retrospective at the end of a project.

The whole lifecycle (retrospective → promotion) is in the reference page [A department's lifecycle](/en/lifecycle).
:::

::: point An honest status: the structure is open, but little has actually been promoted yet.
As of 2026-10-07 the portfolio holds 5 roles and no workflows or insights. There are 2 retrospective records.

Sources: `instance/portfolio/index.yaml`, `instance/retrospectives/` (record exists, repository private).
:::
