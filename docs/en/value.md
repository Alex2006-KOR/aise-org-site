The last category. It revisits **what the structures above were actually worth**, and what this
organization is ultimately aiming at.

## Why read them in this order

A structure's worth shows up at two moments — **when you have to change it**, and **after it's
built**. So the two pages go in that order.

1. **[How the structure absorbs change](/en/structural-principles-ocp)** — whether a design is
   good isn't decided when you add a feature; it's decided by whether you can add it **without
   touching what's already there**. Why this org shaped its adapters, catalog, and role-classes
   that way, and where a rule like "don't factor out a shared module until the third time" came
   from.
2. **[What it is ultimately for](/en/ultimate-goal)** — and what all of this is finally in
   service of. This is where the question of why we call it an "organization" rather than a
   "framework" gets answered.

In one line: **does it hold up when changed → so what is it ultimately trying to build.**
