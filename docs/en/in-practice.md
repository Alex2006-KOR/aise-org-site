# In Practice: Usage & Handoff

The previous two categories explained **what the organization looks like**. This one covers what
actually happens when you **hand it work**, and how that work **survives after the session ends**.

There's a reason these two are bundled into one category. In AISE, "handing over work" and
"leaving a record" aren't separate activities — the act of handing over starts with a record and
ends with a record. Explained apart, both come out as halves.

## Why read them in this order

1. **[How to hand work to this org](/en/usage)** — first, how **a single unit of work** begins.
   The operator declares a mode, speaks to 업무참모, 업무참모 assembles a department, and the PM
   divides it among department members. Up to here it's all about "right now."
2. **[Carrying on across sessions](/en/handoff)** — and then the session ends. Sometimes it hits
   a context limit, sometimes you just close the window. What's left behind, and how the next
   session picks it up. This page includes **the real loss-and-re-derivation incidents this very
   department went through.**

In one line: **how a unit of work begins → what's left once it's over.**
